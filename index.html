About

Quiet conviction.
Measured pace.

Ghost Capital exists for investors who prefer substance to spectacle. We partner with a small circle of limited partners who share a long time horizon and an appetite for careful, independent judgment.

Our work is grounded in research depth and risk discipline—not narrative, not momentum theater. We look for situations where patience and clarity create an edge that noise cannot.

We keep a low profile by design. Capital that moves quietly tends to think more clearly.

Approach

How we work

Four principles that shape every decision we make—quietly, and without exception.

01

Research depth

We favor primary work over consensus. Understanding the structure of a situation comes before sizing a position.

02

Risk first

Drawdowns are managed as carefully as upside. Capital preservation is not a slogan—it is the frame for every allocation.

03

Alignment

Interests travel together. We structure partnerships so incentives stay clear, durable, and free of unnecessary complexity.

04

Patience

Time is a scarce resource we refuse to waste. We wait for setups that reward waiting—and decline the rest.
